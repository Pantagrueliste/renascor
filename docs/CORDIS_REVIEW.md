# EU-funded digital text resources: first review

Reviewed on 9 October 2026 against the 196-entry Renascor catalogue. **Three missing collections publish relevant edited texts:** RUTTER, ItalArt and Forbidden Prayers. Further leads need access, date, geographical or editorial checks. These findings are research candidates, not new live catalogue records.

## Missing published collections

| Collection | EU project | Verified evidence | Remaining work |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [RUTTER Digital Library](https://rutter-project.org/digital-library-shelf-main/) | [833438](https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/833438) | Curated maritime texts with dated entries, readers and XML downloads. *Esmeraldo* is dated c. 1505–1508; its downloaded XML is TEI and declares CC-BY-4.0. | Filter texts dated after 1700 and undated items; check rights across files and count the eligible texts. |
| [ItalArt](http://italart.ovi.cnr.it/) | [794074](https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/794074) | Italian art documents with reading and semidiplomatic editions. A [letter of 28 February 1544](http://italart.ovi.cnr.it/testo/AMP) establishes in-period material. | Date-filter the publisher’s 367 texts; establish text rights. The HTTP site works; HTTPS failed. |
| [Forbidden Prayers Digital Library](https://forpral.uab.cat/en/prayer-library/) | [839195](https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/839195) | Public edited prayers. The [Saint Margaret record](https://forpral.uab.cat/en/prayer/oracion-de-santa-margarita/) includes a full Italian text from a 1581 witness. | Count source witnesses within 1450–1700 and establish rights. Dates of censorship bans must be distinguished from dates of the texts. |

Overlap with texts in another collection would not disqualify these resources. Unknown counts can remain unrecorded while a catalogue entry is reviewed.

Examples include the unavailable Doni archive, LexArt’s edited quotations, planned ARITHMETIC/LAUDARE/Queens’ correspondence releases, and SKILLNET’s correspondence metadata. NOSCEMUS, Post Scriptum and Making and Knowing are already catalogued.

## Coverage and limits

The first pass screened **126,612 project records** from the official FP4–FP7, Horizon 2020 and Horizon Europe bulk datasets. It also screened **907,140 report-summary, deliverable and publication metadata rows** from eight available bulk archives. Direct output screening found 23 historical-text matches; joining text-related outputs to 268 retained projects found 57 further rows. These are search hits, not counts of eligible resources.

Targeted checks requested 52 CORDIS reporting pages: 51 were retrieved, including 43 actual report summaries and eight fallback project pages; the ALFA request timed out. Publisher pages, repositories and sample editions were then checked. The audit records archive URLs, SHA-256 checksums, dataset update dates, screening expressions, project IDs, report-page outcomes and resource-specific evidence.

**This is a metadata-led first pass, not a reading of every attached PDF.** FP1–FP3 and other EU funding schemes are outside the downloaded dataset coverage. Keywords can miss relevant resources, and a planned database or a project’s reported text total does not establish a publicly available, period-filtered edition. Further document-level checking remains necessary for an exhaustive inventory.
